# Northwind Labs Forms and Codes

This list of forms is fictional and exists only to test Shema AI.

Form EXP-204 is used to claim travel expenses such as flights, hotels and taxis.

Form EXP-117 is used to request the purchase of equipment such as monitors, keyboards and chairs.

Form EXP-311 is used to report a lost company credit card.

Form HR-12 is used to apply for parental leave and must be signed by your manager.

Form HR-19 is used to update your bank account details for salary payments.

Form IT-07 is used to request access to a new software tool or system.

Project codes: Project ORCA is the internal name for the mobile app rewrite. Project KESTREL is the data warehouse migration. Project MERIDIAN is the new customer support portal.

The payroll system is called PayStream and the leave system is called LeaveLink.
