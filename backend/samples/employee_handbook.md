# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Northwind Labs is a fictional software company used to test Shema AI. Everything in this handbook is invented for demonstration purposes.

## Working hours

Our standard working week is 40 hours, from Monday to Friday. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, when everyone should be reachable. Outside core hours you can choose when to start and finish, as long as your team knows your schedule.

Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager. Approved overtime is compensated with time off at a rate of one and a half hours for every extra hour worked. Time off from overtime must be used within three months.

## Remote work

Employees may work from home up to three days per week after completing their probation period. During probation, new employees work from the office every day so they can learn from their team.

Remote workers must be available on the company chat during core hours and must join video meetings with their camera on when presenting. The company provides a laptop and a monthly internet allowance of 1,500 rupees for remote work.

Working from another country requires written approval from HR at least 30 days before travel, because of tax and data protection rules.

## Probation

Every new employee has a probation period of six months. Halfway through, your manager holds a review meeting to discuss your progress. At the end of probation, you receive a written confirmation of your permanent position or, in rare cases, an extension of up to three months.

## Leave

New employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave in their first year. From the second year, annual leave increases to 20 days. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; any remaining days expire on 31 March.

Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Your manager approves or rejects the request within five working days.

Sick leave is paid in full for up to 12 days per year. If you are sick for more than two consecutive days, you must upload a doctor's note to the HR portal.

Parents receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave or 4 weeks of paid paternity leave. Adoption leave follows the same rules as maternity and paternity leave.

## Expenses and travel

Business travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Economy class is used for flights shorter than six hours; business class may be approved by a director for longer flights.

Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to 2,000 rupees per day. Keep all receipts and submit your expense report within 30 days of returning. Reports submitted later than 60 days are not reimbursed.

## Learning and development

Each employee has a yearly learning budget of 40,000 rupees for courses, books and conferences. Requests above 15,000 rupees need approval from your manager. If you leave the company within one year of a course paid for by Northwind Labs, you may have to repay part of the cost.

Every Friday afternoon from 15:00 is reserved for learning time. You can use it for online courses, reading, or internal knowledge-sharing sessions.

## IT and security

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and are managed with the company password manager. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for email, chat and code repositories.

Never share your login details with anyone, including the IT team. The IT team will never ask for your password.

Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT help desk within one hour, so the device can be locked remotely. Company data may only be stored on company devices and approved cloud services.

## Leaving the company

The notice period is one month during probation and two months after probation. On your last day, return your laptop, access card and any other company equipment to the office. Your final salary, including unused annual leave, is paid with the next regular payroll.
